Questions about this checklist

Is this a security audit?

No. It is a general checklist of basics, not an assessment of your business. It does not replace advice about the rules that apply to your sector.

Do I need to do everything at once?

No. Start with owning your accounts, email sign-in and backups. Those protect you from the most damaging problems. Work through the rest as the team grows.

Is my progress saved or shared?

Your ticks are stored only in this browser on this device. They are not sent to us or anyone else, and clearing your browser data removes them.

Can you set this up for me?

Yes.
