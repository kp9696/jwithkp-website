    Questions about this estimate

    Is this a quote?

    No. It is a planning estimate based on published list prices. Final costs depend on the plans you choose, discounts, how much you use and exchange rates. We confirm real numbers in a written estimate after a free call.

    Why is open source not always cheaper?

    Open-source tools have no licence fee, but someone has to host, secure and maintain them. For a small team, a cloud server for monitoring can cost more each month than per-user licences. It gets cheaper as the team grows, or when you already own the hardware.

    What is left out?

    Hardware, backup storage, Veeam and enterprise SIEM licences, firewalls, GST and our own fees. Some of these vendors do not publish prices, so we do not guess them.

    How current are the prices?

    Each price was read from the vendor’s page on the date shown in the sources table. Vendors change prices without notice, so treat the figures as a starting point and check the vendor page before buying.
