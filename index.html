Questions we often hear

Short, honest answers. If yours is not here, ask on a free call.

It depends on your team size, the tools you choose and how much needs setting up. Google, Microsoft and most other vendors charge per person per month for their software, and our IT cost estimator shows rough ranges for both enterprise and open-source options. We scope the work on a free call and give you a written estimate before anything starts, so there are no surprises. Firewall, networking, software and website projects are priced the same way: after the free call, with a written estimate before any work starts.
Start with the basics that protect you from the most common problems: business email on your own domain, a password manager with two-step sign-in, secured laptops and backups that have been test-restored. Wi-Fi and a firewall, cloud hosting and security monitoring come next, as your team and your data grow.
Most small teams are better off in the cloud: there is no hardware to buy, it scales easily and costs start small. An office server makes sense when workloads are steady, data has to stay on-site, or cloud bills have grown large. We can size both options for you before you decide.
Google Workspace suits teams that work in the browser and want simple administration. Microsoft 365 suits teams that rely on desktop Word, Excel and Outlook, or need built-in device and security management. If you want your data on servers you control, Nextcloud is an open-source option. We help you pick for your team, and we work with licences you already own.
If your team works from home and in the cloud, laptop security and strong sign-in matter more than an office firewall. Once you have an office, shared servers or a VPN, a business-grade firewall is worth having. We use pfSense or Fortinet depending on your budget and how hands-on you want to be.
The Digital Personal Data Protection Act expects businesses that handle personal data to protect it with reasonable security safeguards. We help with the technical side: who can access data, encryption, backups and incident logging. We do not give legal advice, so check your wider obligations with a lawyer.
We provide business-hours support, with first response within 24 business hours. We do not run a 24x7 security operations centre. Your agreement states the exact support hours and how urgent out-of-hours alerts are handled.
