Who We Are

One technology partner, from first office to custom software

We understand business technology requirements, provide both open-source and commercial solutions, and manage everything from infrastructure to software development.

Why We Exist

JwithKP stands for Journey with Knowledge & Practice. Many teams either overspend on enterprise tools they do not need yet, or run on a patchwork that is not safe. We exist to give founders and growing businesses honest, right-sized IT, with infrastructure, security and software under one roof.

We recommend commercial platforms such as Microsoft 365, Fortinet or Cisco when they fit, and open source such as Proxmox, Wazuh, pfSense or Nextcloud when it is better. The right answer depends on your requirements, security, scalability and budget.

How a Project Runs

We understand your requirements, plan the technology and approach, implement in stages, then secure and test before handover. You get configurations, runbooks and admin access, with credentials handed over securely at the end of the project.

Support runs during agreed hours, with first response within 24 business hours. See the full methodology.

What We Will Not Do
