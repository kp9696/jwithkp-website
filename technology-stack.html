Good to Know

How to evaluate the right technology stack

The right stack is rarely the one with the most features. It is the one that matches the business operating model, the internal support capacity, the required uptime, the security expectations, and the budget available for ongoing ownership. That is why we compare tools by fit, not by trend.

FAQ

Common questions before stack selection

Should the stack be fully open source?

Not always. Many businesses get the best result from a hybrid model that uses open-source where it adds value and commercial tooling where support, compliance, or user expectations matter more.

Can the stack change over time?

Yes. Good architecture makes it easier to start with one model and evolve toward a broader platform strategy as the business grows.
