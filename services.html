How to choose the right IT service scope

Most businesses do not need every service at once. The better starting point is to identify whether the current pressure is recurring licensing cost, migration risk, weak security visibility, collaboration friction, or infrastructure instability. From there, the service scope can stay focused, measurable, and easier to deliver in phases.

Frequently Asked Questions

Enterprise Implementation & Support FAQs

Answers to key questions regarding virtualization cutover, SIEM security, and support SLAs.

Can Proxmox VE completely replace VMware vSphere / ESXi?

Yes. Proxmox VE provides enterprise clustering, KVM hypervisor virtualization, LXC containers, software-defined storage (Ceph/ZFS), live VM migration, and integrated Proxmox Backup Server (PBS) support, reducing hypervisor licensing costs by up to 70%.

How does Wazuh SIEM compare to Splunk or Microsoft Sentinel for SMBs?

Wazuh is an open-source security platform that combines endpoint EDR, real-time log ingestion, vulnerability detection, and file integrity monitoring (FIM) without per-gigabyte log ingestion tax or recurring licensing fees.

What is the typical downtime during an enterprise infrastructure migration?

We execute migrations using a phased 4-stage rollout with proof-of-concept validation, parallel workload syncing, and off-peak cutovers, achieving zero business downtime for core production services.

Do you provide 24/7 SLA-backed managed support?

Yes. JwithKP offers 24/7 L1-L3 engineering support, active threat detection, automated snapshot validations, and SLA-backed response times under NDA.
