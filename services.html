1 · For new teams

Startup Launch Pack: Day-1 IT setup

For founders and teams of up to 50 people who want email, devices, network and backups set up properly from the start, without buying more than they need.

What is included

  • Domain and business email (Google Workspace or Microsoft 365)
  • Device security: encryption, updates and screen lock on your laptops
  • A password manager and two-step sign-in for the team
  • Wi-Fi and firewall set up for your office or home setup
  • Backups, with a restore test
  • Access on and offboarding: a checklist and the accounts set up for it

What is not included

  • Hardware: you provide the laptops, routers and other equipment
  • Ongoing support after the project (see the IT Retainer)
  • Legal advice, including your legal duties under the DPDP Act
  • Custom software, website or ERP development

3 · For existing setups

Cost Optimization & Migration

For businesses already paying for servers, licences or cloud. We review what you run, show where you may be overpaying, and move you in phases with a rollback plan. Savings depend on your setup, so we estimate them with you before any work starts.

VMware to Proxmox migration

Move off VMware ESXi without a full rebuild: a Proxmox VE cluster with live-migration high availability, migrated in phases with a tested rollback plan. Licensing can be significantly lower for suitable setups.

  • Clustered Proxmox VE with Ceph or ZFS storage
  • Proxmox Backup Server for backups
  • Phased migration, planned to avoid business-hours downtime

Splunk to Wazuh

Replace per-gigabyte log licensing with Wazuh for threat detection, file-integrity monitoring and endpoint logs. How it is run day to day is described under Security & Monitoring.

  • Log and endpoint coverage mapped before the move
  • Alert rules rebuilt and tuned with you
  • Old and new running side by side until you are comfortable

Licence and cloud-bill review

Compare what you pay for Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Azure or AWS with what you actually use, and see where a self-hosted option such as Nextcloud makes sense.

  • Right-sizing of licences and cloud resources
  • Nextcloud as a private alternative where it fits
  • Identity integration (LDAP or Active Directory)

Typical migration timeline: around four weeks for a setup of 3–10 servers. Larger or more complex environments take longer, and we confirm the timeline after the discovery phase.

4 · Stay protected

Security Setup & Monitoring

Practical protection for small teams: know when something is wrong, keep an attacker from moving around your network, and be able to restore from a backup.

Managed Wazuh SIEM

Central monitoring of the endpoints and logs it covers, with file-integrity monitoring and vulnerability detection. We agree with you which alerts matter and who acts on them.

  • Threat detection and active-response rules
  • Host intrusion detection and vulnerability scanning
  • Alert rules agreed with you and reviewed during support hours

Firewall & VPN

pfSense or Fortinet firewalls, WireGuard VPN for remote staff and branches, and separate networks for guests and devices so a problem in one place stays there.

  • Firewall rules and guest Wi-Fi separation
  • Encrypted WireGuard access for remote staff
  • Optional intrusion detection (Suricata)

Backup & recovery

Backup copies designed to survive ransomware, on Proxmox Backup Server or Veeam, with restore tests and recovery targets (RPO and RTO) set for each system.

  • Immutable or offline backup copies
  • Regular restore tests
  • Recovery targets agreed per system

Monitoring follows the support terms above. We do not run a 24x7 security operations centre.

Enterprise tools, when they are the right call

We recommend Microsoft 365, Google Workspace, Fortinet, Veeam, AWS or Azure when they fit your team and budget, and open source (Proxmox, Wazuh, pfSense, Nextcloud, WireGuard, Zabbix) when it is better for you. The decision table on our home page shows how we choose.

FAQ

Questions We Hear Before Every Project

Short answers on the Launch Pack, retainers, migration and support.

What is in the Startup Launch Pack?

Domain and business email, device security, a password manager with two-step sign-in, Wi-Fi and firewall, backups with a restore test, and access on and offboarding. You provide the hardware, and software subscriptions are billed directly by the vendors.

Do I need an IT retainer after the setup?

Not always. A very small team can run on a good setup and call us when needed. A retainer makes sense when you want a named engineer to handle everyday requests, joiners and leavers, updates and backup checks every month.

Can Proxmox VE replace VMware vSphere / ESXi?

For many small and mid-sized setups, yes. Proxmox VE provides clustering, KVM virtualization, containers, software-defined storage (Ceph or ZFS), live migration and Proxmox Backup Server support, and can mean significantly lower licensing cost. We check your workloads first, because some environments depend on VMware-specific features.

How does Wazuh compare to Splunk for small teams?

Wazuh is an open-source security platform that combines endpoint detection, log analysis, vulnerability detection and file-integrity monitoring without per-gigabyte ingestion pricing. Splunk offers deeper analytics for very large log volumes, if you have the budget and the staff to run it.

How long does a migration take, and will there be downtime?

For a setup of 3–10 servers a migration typically takes around four weeks. We plan it in phases with a proof of concept and off-peak cutovers, aiming to avoid business-hours downtime. We cannot promise there will be no downtime, and larger environments take longer.

What support do you provide after go-live?

Support is agreed in writing for each project, with first response within 24 business hours. We provide business-hours support and do not run a 24x7 security operations centre. JwithKP is a new, founder-led practice, so you work directly with the engineer who built your environment.
